Frequently asked questions

Quick answers for lenders, closing agents, attorneys and signers.

What areas do you serve?

I’m based in Hudson and travel to Hernando and Pasco counties, within about 25 miles. Remote online notarization is available beyond that range.

What should the signer have ready?

A valid, unexpired government-issued photo ID, and the documents to be signed. Please don’t sign before the appointment; the notarial act has to be performed in my presence.

How does remote online notarization work?

We meet over a secure audio-video session. I verify the signer’s identity, the signer signs electronically, and I apply my electronic notarial seal and signature. You’ll need a computer or smartphone with a camera and microphone, and a reliable internet connection.

Do you accept eDocs?

Yes. Send the package and your instructions in advance, and tell me how you want the signed documents returned.

What are your fees?

I work with banks, lenders, closing agents and attorneys under their established fee structures. Contact me with the details of the signing and I’ll confirm before the appointment.

Are you an attorney? Can you explain the documents?

No. I’m a Florida notary public, not an attorney. I can’t give legal advice, prepare legal documents or explain what a document means. I can confirm identity, witness the signing and notarize as directed.

How do I book?

Call or text (727) 619-4820 or email notary@augello.net with the signer’s details, location or preferred time, and the type of signing.
